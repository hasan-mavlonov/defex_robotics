# v0.2c: controller C on the UR5e arm under poor vision (development)

These are development measurements in simulation, on branch `v0.2c/arm-robustness`, which started from 54f3097 (the frozen Checkpoint 2 code). The final controller code is commit **a5bd452**.

Seeds run:
- 2026: diagnosis and development.
- 4002: validation, with a pre-registered selection rule.
- 2027: confirmation.

No other seed was run, and nothing under `results/benchmark_v0.2b` was read. Every number below is in `tables.md` (generated by `make_tables.sh` from the committed `trials.csv` files) or in the file named next to it. All commands are in `commands.sh`.

## Diagnosis (seed 2026 only)

I used traces (`experiments/trace_trial.py`) and a failure classifier over the committed event logs (`experiments/c_arm_failures.py`).

Classes of C's failures on the arm, seed 2026 standard set, before -> after:

| Class | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Wedged in the cavity | 2 | 1 |
| Identical repeated attempts | 3 | 0 |
| Insert timeout while descending in the cavity | 1 | 0 |
| Protective stop after a failed wall-yaw check | 2 | 0 |
| Other protective stops | 1 | 1 |
| Search (time or retry limit, every attempt blocked on the top face or lead-in) | 5 | 6 |

What the traces showed:

1. **Insert timeout in the cavity.**
   - INSERT's 6 s timeout was set at the gantry's speeds. The W5 speed rule slows contact speeds on the arm to 0.40x, about 4 mm/s.
   - With the noise_2mm depth uncertainty the approach starts 8.5 mm above the estimated mouth. A plug still descending freely, 14.5 mm into the cavity, timed out.
   - It was then diagnosed `no_contact`, and the localizer moved the next probe away from the hole.
   - In the 60-trial dev set, 4 of the 9 trials the gantry seats at the first attempt hit this on the arm.
2. **Wedged plugs.**
   - With dampings scaled for a stable loop, the arm's tilt admittance turns about 0.2 deg/s under the 0.12 N m the walls put on a 3 deg-tilted plug. The gantry wrist turns it about 12 deg/s.
   - So the plug entered tilted and wedged part-way in. The centre of pressure of the reaction was 20-30 mm outside the plug face.
   - The rate-mode seat conform then stopped at once. Its "wall" rule fires on any torque above the face-contact bound, even when the wall pushes the plug the right way. It also turned axes that carried only noise-level torque, in the direction of the noise.
   - Retracting a wedged plug after the failed yaw check tripped the 0.6 N m cell stop.
3. **Identical repeats.** A failed verification was retried "at the same probe" with the same orientation, so it failed the same way until the time limit. The same happened after a torque trip at the floor: the setpoint lead (4.3 mm/s x 0.2 s) hit the floor, and C retracted and repeated.
4. **Wall-yaw check.** Before the change there were 8 failed checks on seed 2026.
   - 5 were on plugs that really were wedged: no free travel, lever arms 30-60 mm, not seated.
   - 3 were one good plug (friction_1 #10, seated, 1.4 deg yaw) checked three times. At mu = 1, floor friction stopped one probe before the wall, so the free travel looked short.
   - Tilt narrowing the free travel was not what failed these checks.
5. **Search.**
   - Of the 24 noise_2mm failures at 9818d18 (60 trials), 16 had a vision tilt error above 3 deg. The gantry seats 13 of those 16.
   - Arm attempts are slower: median 4.0 s against 2.6 s on the gantry. The rate conform alone takes 1.2-1.7 s against 0.1-0.4 s.
   - The arm's tipped-conform CoP misses the contact model by about 3-4 mm median, against 0.13 mm on the gantry. This is from an in-loop check on 10 trials each of noise_1mm and noise_2mm.
   - One cause found: at the tilt limit the plug trails the setpoint by about 0.7 deg. The conform stopped and called it "tipped", the plug then passed flat, and the CoP was read from the far edge.

## What changed

Every new constant is in `AdaptiveConfig` with a basis comment, and every rule is an exact no-op on every gantry.

Gating: all rules except the timeout use the stiff-wrist threshold (`stiff_wrist_min_ratio`, which turns on the rate-mode conform). The timeout uses the W5 contact speed scale, which is exactly 1 on the gantry; the sim-randomization bands do not vary tool mass. `tests/test_fixture_scaling.py` checks that every new flag is off on the nominal gantry, on the randomization-band corners and on the Micro-Fit fixture.

The last column is the number of successes lost when only that rule is switched off, on seed 2026 with 60 trials, in the order noise_1mm / noise_2mm / friction_1 / mf_noise_1mm.

| Rule | Switch | Default | Successes lost with only this rule off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insert timeout x 1/s (UR5e: 6 -> 15 s) | `scale_insert_timeout` | on | 0 / 1 / 0 / 0 |
| Torque or force trip at the floor: hold at the measured depth and diagnose, instead of retracting | `settle_floor_impact` | on | 0 / 0 / 1 / 0 (plus 1 stop and 1 loss on seed 2027) |
| In-cavity tilt compliance: turn tilt at 1 deg/s towards wall torque above 1 mm x axial force | `cavity_tilt_compliance` | on | 1 / 0 / 3 / 3 (latching figure from 75e1585); without it noise_2mm had 1 escape |
| In-place un-jam: on a stall or trip part-way in with wall-level torque, hold a light preload, turn tilt at 2 deg/s towards the torque until relieved, insert again (2 per attempt) | `unjam_in_cavity` | on | 0 / 3 / 0 / 0 |
| Rate conform at the tilt limit waits for the plug before calling it tipped | `conform_limit_waits_for_plug` | on | 1 / 1 / 0 / 0 |
| Failed wall-probe verification is re-aimed with what the attempt learned (not for latching parts) | `reaim_after_verify_fail` | on | 0 / 0 / 1 / - |
| Wall-yaw check probes again once when the two lever arms have opposite signs | `wall_yaw_reprobes` | 1 | 0 / 0 / 1 / - |
| Seat conform follows wall torque along the turn and skips noise-level axes | `seat_conform_follow_walls` | **off** | 0 / -1 / 0 / -: no gain, left off |
| Top-face conform skips noise-level axes | `conform_skip_noise_axes` | **off** | 1 / 0 / 0 / -1: no gain, left off |

With all nine rules off, the code reproduces the untouched code field for field (240 trials, seed 2026). The rules overlap: with all of them on, noise_1mm went from 52 to 59, although no single rule accounts for more than 1 of those.

Which changes came from looking at seed-2026 failures: all of them.
- The re-aim rule was restricted to non-latching parts after it passed one latched plug 3.04 deg tilted on seed 2026. Push-click-pull cannot see tilt.
- The two rules now off were switched off on the seed-2026 ablation.

What carried over to seed 2027: +3 on the standard set (all on noise_2mm, with 1 new failure) and +3 on latching good parts. Neither is significant alone; pooled it is 1 / 7 (p = 0.07).

Two constants of the rate conform went to a pre-registered sweep on validation seed 4002: `rate_conform_settle_deg` (0.03 deg) and `rate_conform_limit_deg` (4 deg). The rule (`validation4002/SELECTION_RULE.md`) was committed before the sweep; the result is in `validation4002/RESULT.md`.

| Candidate | Successes of 120 |
|---|---|
| base (0.03 deg, 4 deg) | 102 |
| settle 0.1 deg | 97 |
| limit 6 deg | 98 |
| both | 100 |

There were no escapes. The rule selected base, so both constants stay at their W5b values.

## The wall-geometry yaw check

I did not build a tilt-aware free-travel model, because on seed 2026 tilt was not what made the check reject good plugs (point 4 above). The check is unchanged except for one re-probe when the lever arms are inconsistent with a rigid yawed plug.

Failed checks, before -> after: seed 2026 8 -> 1, seed 2027 2 -> 0. The wedges that used to fail it are now mostly aligned by the in-cavity rules before the probes run.

The check still cannot pass a plug yawed beyond 2 deg without its one in-place correction, and it still assumes probes along the short axis of a rectangular cavity.

## Open issues

- **Floor-impact hold.** It traded one success on seed 2026 for one stop and one loss on seed 2027. It only drops the axial setpoint lead; the orientation lead is still pushed into the floor. Either drop that too or switch the rule off. Deciding needs a fresh seed.
- **noise_2mm search on the arm is not fixed.** Attempts take about 4 s, and the tipped-conform CoP is far from the contact model. With a large vision tilt error the plug never finds flat within the 4 deg conform range. The validated sweep did not support a wider range or a looser settle threshold.
- C still rejects bad parts only by running out of time, about 26 s each.
- G's arm model was trained on the old C's contacts, so G now runs on a different contact distribution.
- The latching push-click-pull check cannot see tilt (the 3.04 deg case above). Any future retry change for latching parts needs a tilt check first.
- The arm results rely on Menagerie's 0.2 s servo lag. None of this is validated on hardware.
