# v0.2f evidence: where C's remaining edge comes from

(Written as REPORT.txt because writing REPORT.md was blocked in this session. The text is
the intended REPORT.md.)

Analysis of committed results only; no simulation was run. Inputs: `results/benchmark_v0.2d`
(seed 20261123), `results/benchmark_v0.2d_rep` (seed 20261124), both on the v0.2d code
(42df5c8), and `results/benchmark_v0.2e` (seed 20261130, v0.2e code ddd0d34). Every number
below is printed by `python -m experiments.v02f_evidence --out results/v0.2f_evidence`
and listed in `KEY_NUMBERS.md` with the table it comes from. Simulation only.

Labels. POST HOC marks anything chosen after the benchmark results were seen. The whole
per-condition breakdown is post hoc: the pre-registered comparisons are per cell
(machine and suite). No p-value is pooled across seeds, and none is corrected for
multiple comparisons. Bad unit = `declared_success` True and `gt_seated` False.

## Result

1. **C's lead on `standard` comes from three conditions (POST HOC).** On all three seeds,
   the trials that C seats and I (spiral_rcc_pull) does not are in noise_1mm, noise_2mm
   and generalization_combined: gantry 60 of 60, 62 of 62, 53 of 53; arm 49 of 51, 49 of
   51, 53 of 54 (the rest are generalization_tight). On these housing-only parts I runs
   no pull, and its rows equal F's (`CONCENTRATION.md` (b), `PERCOND.md`).
2. **More than half of those wins are I's bad units.** On the C-only trials I passed a
   bad unit (declared success, ground truth not seated) in 44/60, 37/62, 30/53 (gantry)
   and 31/51, 34/51, 30/54 (arm). On the rest, I rejected or aborted
   (`CONCENTRATION.md` (c)). All of I's standard bad units are in the same three
   conditions on every seed (gantry 48, 39, 32; arm 43, 39, 32; `SEEDS.md` (c)).
3. **The bad-unit gap comes from C's VERIFY, not C's insertion.** J runs F's insertion,
   then C's VERIFY. Gantry standard, trials where F passed a bad unit: J seated 48 of 48,
   37 of 39 (1 rejected, 1 bad unit) and 32 of 32. Arm: J seated 28 of 43, 21 of 39 and
   21 of 32, and rejected the rest (`CONCENTRATION.md` (d)). On housing-only parts, C's
   VERIFY corrects the pose: it aligns tilt to the floor and centres yaw. On the 32
   gantry trials of seed 20261130, J's VERIFY aligned tilt by a median 2.79 deg (max
   4.00) and turned yaw on all 32 (median 1.86 deg, max 3.50) (`CONCENTRATION.md` (g)).
   Bad units over 7,400 good-part trials: J 3, 2, 2; F 197, 176, 178
   (`BAD_UNITS.md` (a)).
4. **C's insertion adds a smaller edge on top.** C against J (same VERIFY, different
   insertion) on standard, C-only / J-only: gantry 29/6, 33/4, 32/10; arm 36/19, 39/11,
   43/16. Most of it is in noise_2mm (`CONCENTRATION.md` (a), (d)). J against F (same
   insertion, VERIFY added): gantry 48/13, 37/7, 32/9; arm 28/11, 21/9, 21/10. Both hold
   in the same direction on all three seeds (`SEEDS.md` (c)). J is not a fair baseline.
5. **I's bad units sit just past the 3 deg spec.** Seed 20261130, gantry standard: 32 bad
   units, depth 14.38-14.98 mm (3 short of the 14.5 mm limit), max(|yaw|, tilt)
   3.00-5.53 deg, 8 above 4 deg. Arm: 32 bad units, depth 10.43-14.96 mm, 3.01-6.52 deg,
   7 above 4 deg (`BAD_UNITS.md` (b), (c)). The 3 deg yaw and tilt limits are an assumed
   seating specification (README, "Success tolerances"), applied to a housing-only part.
6. **A looser seating spec shrinks C's standard lead; a tighter one widens it (POST HOC
   rescoring, seed 20261130 only).** The four logged pose columns cannot rescore
   `gt_seated` (10,507 of 288,000 rows disagree). So the conditions where that matters
   were replayed with the missing values logged. The 6,000 replayed rows match
   benchmark_v0.2e exactly. Rescored on gantry standard, C-only / I-only is 53/8 at
   3 deg, 31/10 at 4 deg and 26/10 at 5 deg; on arm standard it is 54/16, 30/17
   (p = 0.079) and 27/18 (p = 0.23). I's bad units fall from 32 to 8 to 3 on each machine
   (arm 7 at 4 deg); C stays at 0. These rescored numbers do not replace the
   pre-registered ones (part 3, `TOLERANCE.md`).
7. **C's tail is its retries.** On seed 20261130, C's p95 / median runs from 1.63 (gantry
   standard) to 2.67 (gantry heldout). It is at or under 1.5 only on gantry latch_spread
   (1.47, 1.47, 1.48 on the three seeds) (`PHASES.md` (c)). On gantry standard, 133 of the
   135 successes slower than 1.5 x median had retried. C's successes with no retry have
   p95 / median 1.11 (`PHASES.md` (d)). The retries sit in the same three
   high-camera-error conditions: 143 of 193 retried successes. Of the 53 wins over I, 41
   retried; their median cycle is 9.51 s, against 5.30 s for all of C's successes
   (POST HOC, `PHASES.md` (d2)).

So, on standard, three things come from the same high-camera-error trials: C's success
lead, C's zero bad units against I's 32, and C's slow tail. The checkpoint report sets
out what each possible claim rests on. It does not pick one.

## 3. Tolerance sensitivity (POST HOC; `TOLERANCE.md`, `replay_20261130/`)

`gt_seated` is `seated` from `evaluate_final_state` in `defex/tasks/insertion.py`. A plug
is seated when all of these hold:
- depth >= socket depth - 0.5 mm;
- |x| and |y| each <= clearance + 0.15 mm;
- tilt <= 3 deg and |yaw| <= 3 deg;
- TCP speed <= 2 mm/s;
- on latching connectors, latched with the latch intact.

Recomputed from `final_depth_mm`, `final_lateral_mm`, `final_tilt_deg` and
`final_yaw_deg` at the as-run tolerances, it **does not** reproduce `gt_seated`. 10,507 of
288,000 rows differ:

- 1,784 rows are seated by the code, but their radius exceeds the per-axis limit. All are
  in the band up to sqrt 2 x the limit: plugs toward a corner of the rectangular cavity.
  The radius cannot tell them from plugs that fail on one axis.
- 8,717 rows on the latching suites pass every logged pose check but are not seated. The
  failing check is latched (or settled), and neither is logged.
- 6 rows on the housing-only suites fail the settled check, which is not logged.

So nothing was rescored from the old columns.

**Exact rescoring from a replay (POST HOC, seed 20261130 only).**

Logging. Commit 9197b9c adds five columns to trials.csv: `final_x_mm`, `final_y_mm`
(socket frame), `final_tcp_speed_mm_s`, `final_latched` and `final_latch_intact`. It
changes no scoring and no behaviour.

What was replayed. The plan was committed before the run (`replay_20261130/RUN_PLAN.md`).
A full replay of standard and heldout was estimated at about 91 minutes at 2 workers, so
only the conditions the old columns cannot rescore were replayed. A row seated as run
passed every unlogged check, so depth and angle alone decide it. A row unseated as run
that passed depth and angle failed an unlogged check, so it stays unseated. Replayed, for
C, F, I and J: standard noise_1mm, noise_2mm and generalization_combined; heldout
mischar_1mm, mischar_2mm (and gantry worst_case); latching mf_noise_1mm; both machines.
That is 6,000 rows. The run took 77.5 minutes, over the 60-minute cap, because the CPU
was shared (`replay_20261130/RUN_LOG.md`).

Checks (`TOLERANCE.md` (1), (2)):
- All 6,000 rows equal benchmark_v0.2e in every committed column, `wall_time_s` excepted.
- `gt_seated` recomputed from the new columns at the as-run tolerances matches on 6,000
  of 6,000.
- In the replayed conditions, the old-columns rule agrees with the exact rule on all
  70,689 (row, tolerance) cases it can decide.
- No row outside the replayed conditions is left undecided.
- At 0.5 mm and 3 deg, the rescoring reproduces the logged `success` and bad units on
  every row.
- `git_dirty` is True on five of the six replay configs. During the replay I edited the
  analysis script and its tests; neither is used by the runner. The row-identity check

## 4. Cycle time by phase (`PHASES.md`)

Phases come from the `state` events in `events.json.gz`, over successful trials. For
every successful trial the phases sum exactly to `cycle_time_s` (largest gap 0 s). The
buckets are approach, align/tare, first insertion, retries, verify, and re-insert after a
failed verify (`phase_times` in the script).

Mean seconds per successful trial, seed 20261130 (`PHASES.md` (a), (b)):

| Cell | C verify | J verify | I pull | C insertion | F insertion | C - F mean cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gantry standard | 1.89 | 2.33 | none (I = F) | 2.44 | 1.38 | +2.94 |
| gantry latching | 3.04 | 3.04 | 3.04 | 3.06 | 2.45 | +3.65 |
| arm standard | 2.49 | 3.29 | none (I = F) | 4.90 | 4.01 | +3.38 |
| arm latching | 3.87 | 3.92 | 3.93 | 5.42 | 4.70 | +4.59 |

- **VERIFY on housing-only parts** costs C 1.89 s (gantry) and 2.49 s (arm) per success.
  On gantry standard that is about two-thirds of C's extra time over F.
- **The pull test** on latching parts costs I 3.04 s (gantry) and 3.93 s (arm), the same
  as C's latching VERIFY. So against I on latching parts, the difference is insertion.
- **C's extra insertion time** over F is +1.06 s on gantry standard (first attempt
  +0.61 s, retries +0.44 s). On the latching suites C's first attempt is shorter than F's
  (gantry -0.77 s) and its retries take longer (+1.38 s). Both machines show the same
  pattern (`PHASES.md` (b)).

**p95 / median** of successful cycles, for every controller and run: `PHASES.md` (c). On
seed 20261130 (standard / latching / heldout / latch_spread):

| Controller | gantry | arm |
|---|---|---|
| C | 1.63 / 2.23 / 2.67 / 1.48 | 1.71 / 1.97 / 2.03 / 1.61 |
| F | 1.88 / 4.62 / 3.50 / 1.57 | 2.08 / 2.64 / 2.67 / 1.50 |
| J | 1.56 / 2.56 / 2.47 / 1.47 | 1.70 / 1.88 / 1.71 / 1.59 |

No controller is at or under 1.5 on standard.

**Why C misses p95 <= 1.5 x median.**
- C's median cycle is a first-attempt success. A retry costs several seconds: on gantry
  standard, the slow successes spent a mean 5.47 s in retries.
- About 10% of C's successes retry (193 of 1,981 on gantry standard; 173 of 1,962 on the
  arm). With more than 5% retrying, the 95th percentile falls inside the retried group.
- Over the successes that did not retry, C's p95 / median is 1.11 on gantry standard,
  1.19 on arm standard, and 1.01-1.16 elsewhere (`PHASES.md` (d)).
- The retried trials are mostly the high-camera-error ones that C wins: on gantry
  standard, 143 of the 193 are in noise_1mm, noise_2mm and generalization_combined
  (POST HOC, `PHASES.md` (d2)).
- Failures do not enter the percentile, but they take cell time. C's failed good-part
  trials took a mean 17.7-24.0 s, mostly ending at the retry or time limit
  (`PHASES.md` (e)).

Retrying less might meet the target, but it would give up part of the success lead. This
analysis did not test that trade-off; it is Hasan's to weigh.

## 5. Across seeds (`SEEDS.md`)

`SEEDS.md` (a) gives every cell's C-only / X-only and its McNemar p on each seed
separately. These hold in the same direction on all three seeds (`SEEDS.md` (a), (c); the
per-condition lines are POST HOC):

- C ahead of I and F on standard, both machines (gantry 60/2, 62/3, 53/8; arm 51/17,
  51/11, 54/16; p < 1e-4 on every seed).
- At least 90% of those wins in noise_1mm, noise_2mm and generalization_combined, and all
  of I's standard bad units in those three conditions.
- More than half of C's standard wins over I are I bad units.
- On standard, J seats more than F and passes fewer bad units, and C seats more than J.
- I seats more than C on heldout_worst_case and heldout_handeye_bias_1.5mm (both
  machines), on arm friction_1 and on gantry latching mf_noise_1mm.
- C passes no bad unit and no bad part.
- G has more discordant wins than C on standard, latching and heldout, on both machines
  (not on latch_spread).
- C makes fewer good units per hour than F on standard (gantry 608, 611, 601 against
  1,128, 1,096, 1,100; arm 374, 384, 377 against 593, 586, 593).

Same direction on all three seeds, but the pre-registered verdict differs by seed:
- gantry latching, F ahead of C in discordant pairs (4/16, 4/19, 6/14; p = 0.012,
  0.0026, 0.12);
- arm heldout, I ahead (50/52, 43/58, 52/60; p = 0.92, 0.16, 0.51, so "matches" every
  time).

Mixed direction:
- gantry heldout (C against I 60/39, 46/41, 48/60);
- arm latching (23/17, 20/20, 19/37).

C's p95 / median is above 1.5 on every run except gantry latch_spread.

## Limits

- Simulation only, on one simulator. Three seeds show which outcomes hold across draws
  of scenarios. They do not test the simulator.
- The per-condition and tolerance analyses are post hoc. They explain the pre-registered
  verdicts; they do not replace them.
- For some bad units in the committed runs, the failed check is partly unknown (per-axis
  lateral, settled, latched), as `BAD_UNITS.md` marks. The replay settles this for seed
  20261130 in the replayed conditions only.
- The tolerance rescoring covers one seed, and it is post hoc.
- Equal effort: F, H and I have no post-seat pose correction. J shows what F gains from
  C's VERIFY. No fair baseline with a conventional seat-and-align step was built. That is
  an open fairness item.
