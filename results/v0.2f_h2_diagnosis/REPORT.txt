# v0.2f h2 diagnosis: why C fails on Micro-Fit unseen grasp and on arm F/T drift

(Written as REPORT.txt: writing REPORT.md was blocked.)

All of this report is post hoc: it was decided after the heldout2 results
(`results/heldout2_v0.2f`) were read. It is diagnosis only. No controller code changed, no reported
run was made, nothing was tuned or selected. The counterfactual replays below change one setting or
one sensor on a single named trial to test a mechanism; they are not fix candidates, and no value in
them was searched for.

Code (commit 6bdd4b4 and the commit that adds this file): `experiments/v02f_h2_trace.py` (replay with
the true contact force and depth next to C's view), `experiments/v02f_h2_drift_axes.py` (each drift
trial's drift direction in the controllers' frame, from its seed; no simulation),
`experiments/v02f_h2_dev_tables.py` (development-seed tables from trials.csv and events),
`experiments/trace_trial.py` (now knows the heldout2 conditions), `tests/test_v02f_h2_diagnosis.py`.
Seed 20261207 is replayed only for trials that `results/heldout2_v0.2f/*/trials.csv` lists as C
failures; the script refuses any other. D and F were replayed on the same named trial indices, for
comparison.

## Result

1. Micro-Fit unseen grasp. The told 0.3 mm sigma is part of it, but not the main cause: C's search
   stops moving. With the same sign, the unseen slip and the in-hand rotation put the true tip
   0.82 mm from where C's in-hand estimate puts it; with opposite signs they cancel (0.02 mm). C
   computes the centre of pressure (CoP) about its estimated tip, so a plug resting on one edge reads
   a CoP 3.75-4.16 mm off centre, outside the plug face (half-width 3.43 mm + 0.3 mm margin). DETECT
   then relabels the top-face block as an in-cavity jam (`partial_jam`), even at 0.3-0.5 mm depth.
   RECOVER's jam branch tells the localizer the plug *entered* at that probe and retries from the
   same place. The attempts repeat until the retry limit (gantry) or the time limit (arm). F's spiral
   never uses the CoP or a prior: it passes over the offset and finds the hole by depth.
2. F/T drift on the arm. Neither DETECT nor the bottoming signature is wrong: in all 6 traced bad
   units the plug was at the floor (14.86-15.00 mm true) when C saw the bottoming force. VERIFY then
   pulled it out. VERIFY holds a 1 N axial preload on the force reading for 2-4 s. When the zero has
   drifted more than 1 N in the push-out direction since C's last tare, 1 N on the reading is about
   0 N or a pull in truth, and the plug rises 0.6-1.8 mm during the wall probes. The wall probes
   still pass (the walls are there 13-14 mm down), and nothing re-checks the depth before SUCCESS.
   All 8 reported bad units have an axial drift of +0.19 to +0.30 N/s in the push-out direction.
   A lateral drift does a second thing: the lateral admittance, which zeroes the lateral reading,
   presses the plug on a wall; the wall probes then measure no free travel, and the wall-yaw check
   fails a seated plug at every retry until the time limit. D runs no low-force phase after the
   seat, so a 1-2 N error never becomes a pull. It happens only on the arm because C's contact moves
   are slower there: C reaches the probes 5-8 s after its tare, against 2-4 s on the gantry, and
   only on the arm does the error pass the 1 N preload.
3. Both mechanisms reproduce on development seeds 2026 and 2027. Mode 1: all 24 C failures show the
   CoP relabel and the jam loop, and all are same-sign trials. Mode 2: 6 C bad units, all on the
   arm at 0.3 N/s, all with push-out axial drift; the 8 other C failures there all end with a
   wall-yaw FAIL. Gantry: 0 C failures in 160 drift trials.
4. Neither mode shows in the committed standard or latching data: no C bad unit and no same-probe
   jam loop there. The jam loop does occur in the W2 `heldout` suite, by another route (below).

## Mode 1: `h2_mf_grasp_unseen` (Micro-Fit, plug off in the fingers)

### Traces (seed 20261207, named C failures)

Traced: gantry trials 2, 9, 37 and arm trials 2, 4 of `h2_mf_grasp_unseen_2deg` (all same sign).
The replays reproduce trials.csv (gantry 2: `aborted:retry_limit`, 7 retries, final depth -3.04 mm;
arm 2: `aborted:time_limit`, final -2.78 mm; arm 4: `aborted:time_limit`, final 7.35 mm).

Gantry trial 2 (slip -0.4 mm, rotation -2 deg; arm trial 2 is the same scenario and the same story,
slower):

- Attempt 1: C's view (0, 0) mm; truth (+0.68, -1.29) mm off the socket axis (vision error plus the
  unseen tip offset). The plug stops on the top face. The conform settles flat and measures
  (+0.85, -1.72) deg, which C folds into its socket-tilt estimate. This absorbs most of the in-hand
  rotation; it is not the failure. CoP (+0.26, -0.28) mm: a surface block. The localizer moves the
  probe 0.25 mm, to (-0.25, 0).
- Attempt 2: the conform tips to the 4 deg limit. CoP (-0.04, -3.55) mm, still a surface block. The
  probe moves to (-0.50, +0.25).
- Attempts 3-8: CoP -3.78 to -4.04 mm in y, past the 3.73 mm face bound (`_surface_consistent`,
  `surface_cop_margin_mm` = 0.3). DETECT logs "contact signature inconsistent with the top face:
  treated as in-cavity jam" at a depth of 0.35-0.37 mm, far short of the 1.8 mm engage threshold.
  The `partial_jam` branch of `_plan_correction` calls `localizer.update_entered(probe)`, adds the
  lateral admittance offset (about 0: a tipped face gives no lateral force) and re-aims the tilt.
  Each attempt starts within 0.05 mm of the last. One surface block in between (attempt 5) moved the
  probe to (-0.50, +0.50); then the loop resumed. To the end, the true tip stayed 0.82-1.07 mm off
  the axis in y.
- Gantry trial 9: the first stop is already relabelled (CoP +3.96 mm), so the localizer never moves:
  8 attempts at (-0.04, -0.01 to -0.03) mm. Gantry 37 and arm 4: the same loop from attempt 2.

F on gantry trial 2: face contact at 2.03 s, then the spiral. It found the hole with a 0.30 mm push
at (-0.54, +1.03) mm and seated at 6.07 s, with 0 retries. The needed correction was about
(-0.68, +1.29) mm.

### Which of the candidate causes

Single-trial counterfactual replays, diagnosis only (`--seen-grasp`, `--cfg NAME=VALUE`):

| Replay of C | gantry 2 | gantry 9 | gantry 37 | arm 2 | arm 4 | arm 37 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| as reported | fail | fail | fail | fail | fail | fail |
| in-hand estimate sees the true grasp | seated | seated | seated | seated | seated | not run |
| `surface_cop_margin_mm` 0.3 -> 1.0 | seated (6 retries) | seated | seated | fail | not run | protective stop |
| `min_prior_sigma_mm` 0.15 -> 1.0 (prior 1.3 mm) | seated | fail | seated | seated | not run | seated |

- The in-hand estimate is the root cause. The unseen offset puts C's CoP reference point 0.8 mm off,
  which pushes the edge CoP outside the face, and it adds 0.8 mm to the lateral error the search
  must cover. With the true grasp seen, 5 of 5 replays seat.
- The CoP relabel and the jam branch are what stop the search. Widening only the CoP margin seats 3
  of 5 replays. On arm trial 2 C then reached the right probe, (-0.75, +1.25) mm, at attempt 5 but
  still blocked there; the repeated blocks started the yaw search (+3 deg, then -3 deg), which used
  up the time.
- The search size from the told sigma contributes, as the heldout2 report guessed. C's prior sigma
  is hypot(1.3 x 0.3 mm, 0) = 0.39 mm, and C moves 0.25-0.56 mm per block. The needed 1.1-1.5 mm
  lies about 3-4 prior sigmas out. A wider prior seats 4 of 5 replays, but not gantry 9, where every
  stop is relabelled and the localizer is never consulted.
- The tilt from the in-hand rotation is not a separate cause. The first flat conform measures it
  and folds it in. Its contribution is the 0.42 mm it moves the tip.
- C is never told any in-hand uncertainty. No code in `defex/control` or `defex/recovery` reads
  `grasp_xy_sigma_mm` or `grasp_rot_sigma_deg`. Telling C a larger grasp sigma would change nothing
  without a design change.

Why the sign matters (consistent with the heldout2 post-hoc counts; at 2 deg on each machine: same
sign C 6 of 24 seated, opposite sign 16 of 16): opposite signs leave the tip 0.02 mm from the
estimate, so the edge CoP stays inside the face and the search stays small.

## Mode 2: `h2_ft_drift_0.3` on the arm

### Traces (seed 20261207, named C failures)

Traced: bad units 4, 12, 19 and 28; time-limit failures 3 and 25; protective stop 20; D on trial 12;
F on trial 29. Each replay reproduces trials.csv (for example trial 12: false accept, 9.40 s,
0 retries, final depth 13.05 mm).

C's only tare in these trials is at 1.80 s, in free space just before INSERT. C re-tares at the ALIGN
of every attempt, and these bad units were all first attempts. The "error" below is C's reading minus
the true contact force along the socket axis.

| Trial | at bottoming: true depth, error | VERIFY preload start, then probe start: true / read Fz | true depth at SUCCESS | final depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 14.88 mm, +1.20 N | 4.58 / 6.12 N, then -0.48 / +1.05 N | 14.29 mm | 14.21 mm |
| 12 | 14.86 mm, +1.38 N | 4.45 / 6.17 N, then 0.00 / +1.89 N | 13.23 mm | 13.05 mm |
| 19 | 14.97 mm, +1.31 N | 4.78 / 6.10 N, then -0.17 / +1.33 N | 14.32 mm | 14.19 mm |
| 28 | 14.97 mm, +1.22 N | 4.89 / 6.15 N, then -0.40 / +0.98 N | 14.47 mm | 14.40 mm |

In each bad unit:

- INSERT's bottoming signature fired at the floor (correct). DETECT called a seated candidate
  (correct).
- VERIFY's seat conform ran, then `preload`: `_hold_preload` drives the *read* axial force to
  `verify_preload_n` = 1.0 N. With +1.3 to +1.9 N of error, that is -0.5 to 0 N in truth. During the
  two wall probes and the centring (about 1.5 s) the error grows to +1.7 to +3.0 N and the plug
  climbs. In trial 12 it rose from 15.00 to 13.23 mm.
- The wall probes pass (reactions 2.0-3.1 N, both walls found), because a plug 13-14 mm deep is
  still between the walls. The wall-yaw check passes. SUCCESS. No step after the probes compares the
  encoder depth with the depth at bottoming.

Which check passes wrongly? None of the three misreads a short plug. The plug was seated when the
depth and the bottoming force were checked. VERIFY itself unseated it, and the wall probes cannot see
depth. The assumption that breaks is that VERIFY's force reading is still zeroed: the 1 N preload and
the 2 N wall threshold are both smaller than the error after 5-8 s of drift.

The drift direction, from `v02f_h2_drift_axes` (arm `h2_ft_drift_0.3`, all 40 trials, post-hoc
grouping, no simulation):

| Axial drift (C's frame) | trials | C seated | C bad units | C other failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| push-out, > +0.1 N/s | 13 | 1 | 8 | 4 (2 time limit, 2 protective stop) |
| within +/-0.1 N/s | 16 | 12 | 0 | 4 (time limit) |
| into the socket, < -0.1 N/s | 11 | 8 | 0 | 3 (time limit) |

All 8 bad units have +0.19 to +0.30 N/s. D seated 40 of 40 across the three groups; F and I seated 39.

The other two routes from the same drift:

- Time limit (11 trials; traced 3 and 25). A lateral drift (0.14-0.30 N/s in the plane) makes the
  lateral admittance, which zeroes the lateral reading, press the plug on the walls with a true force
  the size of the drift. Trial 25 at the probes: read (-0.03, +0.09) N, true (-1.83, -1.35) N. Both
  probes stop at once (0.25-0.27 mm of setpoint travel). The measured free travel between the walls
  is 0.001-0.07 mm against the 0.293 mm needed, and `_wall_yaw_verdict` FAILs a plug seated at
  14.72 mm true. RECOVER retries, each retry ends the same way, and the time limit stops it with the
  plug retracted above the mouth. In trial 3 the axial drift is into the socket (-0.19 N/s). There
  the true preload is 2.4-2.8 N instead of 1 N, which adds floor friction to the probes.
- Protective stop (2 trials; traced 20). The force drift, acting at the 50-60 mm lever from the
  sensor to the tip, reads as a tilt torque (DETECT CoP (+6.78, -1.58) mm on a seated plug). The seat
  conform turns the plug towards it, to 3.4 deg, and the wall reaction trips the 0.6 N m stop.

Why D does not suffer. D tares at the same time (1.80 s). But D declares the seat as soon as the
encoder depth window is met at 0.8 x its insertion force or more (5-8 N on the arm), and the trial
ends there. In trial 12, D seated at 4.97 s, true depth 14.96 mm, with a read error of +1.26 N. D has
no low-force phase after the seat and no lateral probes, so a 1-2 N error only changes how hard D
pushes. F's one bad unit (trial 29, axial +0.28 N/s) takes F's own route: during the free descent the
false force reading passed F's contact threshold. F took the still-sliding plug for the floor at
14.43 mm true, and it ended at 14.498 mm, 0.002 mm short of the limit.

J (F's insertion plus C's VERIFY) shows how much of this comes from VERIFY. J passed 5 bad units in
the same condition, all with axial drift above +0.1 N/s. It also had 9 `aborted:verify_failed`
results, which include trials 3, 6, 9, 25 and 33, where C hit the time limit (heldout2 trials.csv,
post hoc).

Why only the arm, and only at 0.3 N/s. C's contact speeds are scaled down on the arm. INSERT took
about 4.1 s on the arm against 1.8 s on the gantry, and VERIFY 2.4-3.4 s against about 1.9 s. At the
probes the error is about 0.3 N/s x 5-7 s = 1.5-2.1 N on the arm (times the axial share of the
drift), against at most about 1 N on the gantry. At 0.1 N/s the arm error stays under about 0.8 N,
and C seated 40 of 40. In a gantry trace on development seed 2026 (trial 12) the axial error at the
probes was -0.6 to -1.4 N, and every phase passed.

## Can either mode occur in the standard or latching suites?

From the committed `results/benchmark_v0.2e` trials.csv and events.json.gz (post hoc):

- Mode 2 needs an F/T error that builds up after C's tare. In standard, latching and latch_spread
  the bias is constant and C tares it out at every attempt. The residual axial error in the traces
  is 0.1-0.3 N (filtering at high forces). C passed 0 bad units in 7,400 good-part trials (v0.2e
  report). C's 4,734 declared successes on the housing part (standard and heldout) all end within
  0.30 mm of the floor: 16 end below 14.9 mm, 1 below 14.7 mm (arm standard `noise_2mm` trial 14,
  14.70 mm), none below 14.5 mm. So C's VERIFY did not measurably lift seated plugs there. A real
  sensor does drift, so this protection comes from the simulation, not from C.
- Mode 1, the CoP relabel: 4 C trials in standard (3 gantry, 1 arm; 5 events), none in latching or
  latch_spread. None of them looped, and none failed.
- The same-probe jam loop (three or more consecutive "jam: re-aim" retries from the same probe): no
  C trial in standard, latching or latch_spread on either machine, except 1 in arm latching
  (`mf_stubbed_terminal`, a bad part) and 1 in arm latch_spread (`mf_terminal_max`). In the W2
  `heldout` suite it ended 17 of 93 gantry and 18 of 116 arm C failures, 31 of them in
  `heldout_mischar_2mm` (true vision sigma 2 mm, told 0.5 mm). There the camera's depth error, not
  the CoP, makes a top-face stop at "3.69 mm" read as engaged, and the same jam branch keeps the same
  probe. This is the same RECOVER weakness, reached by another route.

## What a real cell would do

Integrator settings that a fair comparison would grant to every controller:

- Re-zero the F/T in free space as late as possible before contact. C (at the ALIGN of every
  attempt) and D-J (at TARE) already do this, at about 1.8 s. It does not help here, because the
  error builds during contact, after the last moment in free space. A sensor with less drift
  (warm-up, temperature-compensated gauges, a cable dressed so its drag does not change) helps all
  of them.
- An in-hand camera, or fingers that centre the plug. This removes mode 1's cause for every
  controller: D and F aim with the in-hand estimate too. In the replays C then seated 5 of 5.
- A characterized in-hand accuracy, given to every controller. F and D would not use it (their
  spiral size is fixed), and C does not use it today either.

C design changes (not implemented; for a later, pre-registered round):

- Mode 2: in VERIFY, do not let the preload loop withdraw the plug. For example, hold the encoder
  depth found at bottoming during the probes (a position hold with a force ceiling) instead of a
  force target, or cap the upward travel at about 0.1 mm. Re-check the encoder depth against the
  bottoming depth just before SUCCESS, and re-seat or fail if it rose. C can also estimate the drift
  at no extra cost: it tares before every attempt, so two tares give a drift rate. The same depth
  re-check would fix J; D-I do not need it.
- Mode 1: a stop short of the engage depth that is relabelled only by its CoP should update the
  localizer as a block (with the unclean CoP sigma, or with no CoP), not as an entry. Widen the CoP
  face bound and the localizer prior by a told in-hand uncertainty, once C is told one. After two
  attempts from the same probe without progress, fall back to a bounded spiral, as F does.

Fairness: the depth re-check and the jam-branch fix are C changes that D-F cannot use. An in-hand
camera and a lower-drift sensor are integrator settings, for all controllers.

## Limits

- Traces on the reported seed: 5 of the 36 mode-1 failures at 2 deg; 4 of the 8 bad units and 3 of
  the 11 other mode-2 failures. On the development seeds the event-log counts cover every failure.
- The counterfactual replays are single trials, chosen because they failed. They show what each
  factor does in those trials, not a rate.
- The drift is mapped to the socket frame on the assumption that the F/T axes are the estimated
  socket axes at the insertion pose (identity grasp rotation, sensor parallel to the TCP). The traced
  axial errors agree with it to within about 0.2 N (trial 12: +0.298 N/s x 4.1 s = 1.22 N predicted,
  1.38 N read at bottoming, which includes the filtered contact transient).
- The drift is linear, and its rates are assumed (heldout2 PREREGISTRATION.md).
- No events file exists for the reported heldout2 run, so on seed 20261207 the time-limit route was
  confirmed only in the traced trials.
- Committed per development run: trials.csv, summary.json, summary.csv, config.json, and
  events.json.gz (about 30 KB each). The events files are needed for the jam-loop, CoP-relabel and
  wall-yaw counts in the development-seed tables.
